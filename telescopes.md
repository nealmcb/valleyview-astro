# The Valley View telescopes

Three telescopes live in the roll-off shed at the
[Orient Land Trust](https://www.oltio.org/) / Valley View Hot Springs.

## 20" PlaneWave CDK20

The main instrument. A **508 mm (20-inch)** mirror, f/7.77,
[Dall-Kirkham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dall%E2%80%93Kirkham_telescope)
optical design (a PlaneWave [CDK20](https://planewave.com/)), on a computer-driven
PlaneWave L-500 equatorial mount that slews at up to 50° per second. It reaches
past **20th magnitude** and carries a cooled CCD camera for imaging. This is the
scope for galaxies, nebulae, planets up close, and tracking satellites.

## Celestron C8 — "Orange"

An **8-inch (203 mm)**
[Schmidt-Cassegrain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schmidt%E2%80%93Cassegrain_telescope)
on a motorized mount for tracking, near the north side of the pad. Point it by
hand with the finder, then let the drive follow the sky.

## Celestron NexStar 8 Evolution

Another **8-inch** Schmidt-Cassegrain, at the south end, with a computerized
GoTo mount and built-in WiFi (drive it from a phone). Its battery runs about two
nights per charge.
