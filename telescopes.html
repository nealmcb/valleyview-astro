The Valley View telescopes

Three telescopes live in the roll-off shed at the Orient Land Trust / Valley View Hot Springs.

20" PlaneWave CDK20

The main instrument. A 508 mm (20-inch) mirror, f/7.77, Dall-Kirkham optical design (a PlaneWave CDK20), on a computer-driven PlaneWave L-500 equatorial mount that slews at up to 50° per second. It reaches past 20th magnitude and carries a cooled CCD camera for imaging. This is the scope for galaxies, nebulae, planets up close, and tracking satellites.

Celestron C8 — “Orange”

An 8-inch (203 mm) Schmidt-Cassegrain on a motorized mount for tracking, near the north side of the pad. Point it by hand with the finder, then let the drive follow the sky.

Celestron NexStar 8 Evolution

Another 8-inch Schmidt-Cassegrain, at the south end, with a computerized GoTo mount and built-in WiFi (drive it from a phone). Its battery runs about two nights per charge.

Full operating procedures (rolling the shed, startup and shutdown, software) are in the observatory’s internal Valley View telescopes document.
